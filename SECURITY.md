# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities privately by emailing [security@slb4j.org](mailto:security@slb4j.org). Do 
not open a public issue or disclose the vulnerability publicly before the maintainers have had an opportunity to investigate and address it.

Please include:

- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- The affected SLB4J version and modules, and any relevant configuration.
- Steps to reproduce the issue, including a minimal example or proof of concept if possible.
- Any suggested mitigations or additional information that could help the investigation.

Please avoid including sensitive data in your report. The maintainers will investigate and coordinate next steps and disclosure with you.
